Privacy policy

RenkuOS EMail (the e-mail support built into RenkuOS). Last updated 22 November 2026.

In short

RenkuOS EMail runs entirely on your own computer. It talks only to your mail provider (for a Gmail account, Google). There is no RenkuOS EMail server, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing about you or your mail is sent to the developers or to anyone else.

What it accesses

With your permission, given on Google's own sign-in page, RenkuOS EMail uses the Gmail scope https://mail.google.com/ to read your mail and its labels over IMAP, to change them when you read, star, label, archive or delete a message, and to send the mail you write over SMTP.

Where it is kept

  • Your mail is stored on your computer, in your home folder, as files you can see and delete.
  • Your sign-in (the token Google gives after you agree) is stored in your computer's Key Store. Your Google password is never seen or stored by RenkuOS EMail: you type it only on Google's page.

Sharing

RenkuOS EMail does not share, sell or transfer your data. Your mail goes only between your computer and Google's mail servers, over encrypted connections. It is not used for advertising, and no person reads it.

Google API Services

RenkuOS EMail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Remove the account in E-mail preferences, or sign out there, to stop RenkuOS EMail using it.
  • Revoke its access at any time on your Google Account's third-party connections page.
  • Delete the account's mail folder to remove the mail stored on your computer. The mail on Google's servers is not changed.
