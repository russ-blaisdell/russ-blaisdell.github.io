RenkuOS EMail

The e-mail support built into RenkuOS: its mail daemon, the Mail app and the E-mail preferences.

What it does with Google

RenkuOS EMail lets a Gmail account be used on a RenkuOS computer. When you add a Gmail account, the E-mail preferences open Google's own sign-in page in your browser. After you agree there, RenkuOS EMail uses the permission you gave to:

  • download your mail and its labels over IMAP, so you can read it on your computer;
  • keep your computer and Gmail in step when you read, star, label, archive or delete a message;
  • send the mail you write, over SMTP.

It asks for one permission, https://mail.google.com/, because that is the only one Gmail's IMAP and SMTP servers accept.

Where your data goes

Only between your computer and Google. There is no RenkuOS EMail server: your mail and your sign-in are never sent to the developers or to anyone else. Details are in the privacy policy.
